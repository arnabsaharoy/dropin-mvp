DropIn
the warm glow of a city at night
when you've found your people.

A design language for the newcomer. Everyone else in this market is bright, airy, and ironic. DropIn is dusk: warm, human-lit, and earnest — the city stays dark until your people light it up.

The risk we're taking: going fully warm, dark, and grainy in a category of clean white maps.

02 Light, not color

One light, three heats

The old three-color mode system (green / orange / violet) got called "overused." So we killed it. A Drop's mode isn't a different hue — it's the same warm light at a different temperature. Hotter means more alive.

07 The commit

A button that's lit, not flat

Testers disliked the old flat CTA. Same amber — different physics. The commit glows like it's giving off heat, so the one action that matters looks like the warmest thing on screen. The verb changes with the moment.

Live → "join" · Recent → "count me in" · Memory → "shall we"
